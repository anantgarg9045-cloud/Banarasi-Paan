Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer delivery?

Yes! We offer home delivery within Varanasi. Orders can be placed by phone or through our contact form.

Can I place bulk orders?

Absolutely! We offer special pricing for bulk orders for events and celebrations. Contact us for details.

What are your prices?

Our paan ranges from ₹20 for classic varieties to ₹100 for premium selections. See our products page for details.

Are your ingredients natural?

Yes! We use 100% natural and organic ingredients. No artificial flavoring or preservatives.

How long can paan be stored?

Our paan is best enjoyed fresh. We recommend consuming within 2-3 hours of purchase for optimal taste.

Do you accept special requests?

Yes! Contact us for custom paan preparations for special occasions or dietary preferences.
