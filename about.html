Our Story

A journey of tradition, passion, and authentic craftsmanship

A Living Tradition

Banarasi Paan is more than just a betel leaf preparation - it's a symbol of Varanasi's rich cultural heritage and a testament to centuries of culinary excellence.

Since 1947, our family has been crafting paan with the same dedication and passion that our ancestors brought to this ancient art form. Every paan we make carries with it the essence of Banaras, the spiritual heart of India.

🏛️

Our Journey Through Time

1947 - The Beginning

Our founder, Shri Ram Bahadur, opened the first Banarasi Paan shop in the heart of Varanasi with a simple dream - to serve authentic paan to the people.

1975 - Recognition

Our paan gained recognition across Varanasi, becoming a favorite among locals and visitors seeking authentic taste.

2000 - Expansion

We expanded our operations while maintaining our commitment to quality and tradition, opening multiple outlets across the city.

2024 - Digital Era

Bringing our authentic taste to the digital world while preserving our traditional methods and values.
